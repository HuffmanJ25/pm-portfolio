# V1: Starting point (Nov 20, 2026)

**Tag:** `v1` (commit `c583ab4`)

The stock Astro blog template, scaffolded with `npm create astro@latest -- --template blog`.

## State
- Generic "Hello, Astronaut!" home page, sample blog posts, placeholder About page.
- No personal content, photo, resume or case studies.
- Placeholder social links (Mastodon, Twitter, GitHub) and "Astro Blog" branding.

## Why start here
- **Speed to a working baseline:** a building, deployable site in minutes, so effort goes
  into content and experience rather than plumbing.
- **Proven foundations:** Markdown/MDX content, RSS, sitemap and image optimization out of the box.
- **Static output:** fast, cheap to host (GitHub Pages), nothing to maintain server-side.

## Problems V2 needs to solve
- It doesn't say who Joel is or what he does in the first five seconds.
- No evidence of product impact (outcomes, metrics, decisions).
- No way to get an up-to-date resume.
