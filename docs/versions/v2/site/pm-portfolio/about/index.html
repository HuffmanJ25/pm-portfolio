About

A product manager who started on the ground floor

My first degree was in wind energy and turbine technology, a hands-on field about complex systems and the people who keep them running. Later I added a B.A.S. in IT Management and moved into software.

I started at Cendyn in data quality, validating hotel property-management integration data. That work showed me how small data problems turn into big customer problems. I moved into business analysis and then product ownership, spending time at hotel front desks to watch how staff actually used our product before deciding what to build next.

After a stint as a product analyst at American Express, I spent four years at Corporate Travel Management owning a global traveler-profile platform: consolidating three regional systems into one product for thousands of companies and millions of travelers, with the identity, permissions and compliance work that comes with that scale.

How I work

  • Start with the people doing the work. The best roadmap input I’ve had came from watching someone struggle with a screen.
  • Make the business case explicit. Build vs. buy, cost of delay, savings: I write the numbers down so the decision can be challenged.
  • Ship in stages. Feature flags, beta groups and the willingness to pause something that isn’t working.
  • Use AI as a daily tool. For discovery, diagnosing issues and building working prototypes, like this site.

What I’m looking for

Open to Product Manager roles in identity, data platforms and AI-enabled workflow tools. I’m especially interested in teams where platform work, data quality and good user experience have to meet.
