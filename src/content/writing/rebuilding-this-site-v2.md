This site is a product, so I'm building it like one: in versions, with a reason for every change. I'm keeping screenshots and a working snapshot of each version so I can show how it evolved.

## V1: a fast baseline

V1 was the stock Astro blog template. Getting a site that builds and deploys in minutes meant my time could go into content and experience instead of plumbing. But it said nothing about me.

## What V2 had to solve

- **Who is this, in five seconds?** A visitor should see my name, my photo, what I do and what I'm looking for without scrolling.
- **Show impact, not duties.** Every role and project leads with an outcome.
- **Never send a stale resume.** My resume changes often, so the download button pulls the live version.

## Decisions and tradeoffs

- **Warm over slick.** I chose a warm, personal look over a techy dashboard. Hiring is about people, and I want this to feel like meeting me.
- **An interactive, horizontal timeline.** It's more engaging on a laptop, where most recruiters read. On phones it becomes a vertical list, because dragging sideways on a small screen is frustrating.
- **Live PDF, curated timeline.** The PDF always comes straight from my resume. The timeline is a short, curated summary that I refresh when the resume changes, instead of an automatic import that would break whenever I reformat the document.
- **Static and simple.** The site is plain HTML built ahead of time and hosted for free. The only JavaScript is the small script that runs the timeline.
